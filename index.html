Privacy Policy for linkedin.ai

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how linkedin.ai ("the App") handles information when you use the application to connect with LinkedIn and manage LinkedIn content.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize the App through LinkedIn, the App may access information permitted by LinkedIn's authorization system, such as your LinkedIn member identity and information required to create or manage content on your behalf.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through LinkedIn is used only to provide the features requested by you, including creating and publishing LinkedIn content after you authorize the action.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share LinkedIn information with third parties except where necessary to operate the requested service, comply with applicable law, or protect the security and integrity of the App.

4. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's functionality or meet legal and security requirements. You may request deletion of information associated with the App by contacting us.

5. LinkedIn Authorization

You can revoke the App's access to your LinkedIn account through LinkedIn's account settings. After authorization is revoked, the App will no longer be able to use the revoked permissions.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
